FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do I need an account?

No. FreedomPoint has no accounts and no server of its own. Open it and start. Your data lives on your iPhone and, if you have iCloud turned on, in your own private iCloud so your devices stay in sync.

Does it connect to my bank or broker?

No, and it never asks for your credentials. You enter your holdings by hand or import them from an Excel file, so you stay in control of what the app knows.

Can you see my financial data?

No. Your portfolio is stored in your private iCloud database, which the developer has no access to. There is no analytics and no tracking in the app. The privacy policy lists every case where anything leaves your device.

Where do the quotes come from?

From Yahoo Finance. The app sends the ticker symbols (and currency pairs for conversion) to fetch prices. Prices can be delayed or unavailable, so treat them as informative, not as a trading feed.

What do the AI features do, and what do they cost?

AI suggestions, news summaries and stock analysis are optional. You choose Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini and add your own API key, which stays in your device's Keychain. Usage is billed by that provider, not by us. The privacy policy explains exactly what each feature sends.

Is this financial advice?

No. FreedomPoint is a tracking and planning tool. Projections are estimates built on the assumptions you set, and the plan and AI suggestions are not investment, tax or legal advice. Past returns do not guarantee future ones.

Which languages and currencies are supported?

The app is in English and Spanish, independent of your phone’s language. You pick the currency your net worth is shown in, and holdings in other currencies are converted.
