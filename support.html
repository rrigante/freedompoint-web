FreedomPoint Support

Help with FreedomPoint for iPhone.

Questions, bug reports or feedback: support@freedompoint.app. Please include your iOS version and, if you can, what you were doing when the problem happened. We aim to reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud if iCloud is on, so your devices stay in sync. FreedomPoint has no account system and no server of its own. See the privacy policy.

My quotes are not updating.

Pull down on a screen to refresh. Quotes come from Yahoo Finance and can be delayed or temporarily unavailable. Check your connection and try again in a few minutes.

How do I import from Excel?

Open the More menu › Settings, and under the Excel section choose your workbook. The Broker, RSU and other-asset sheets are read on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How do the AI features work?

They are optional. In Settings you pick Anthropic (Claude) or Google Gemini and paste your own API key. The key stays in your device’s Keychain. See the privacy policy for what each feature sends.

How do I share my portfolio with my partner?

In Settings, use the sharing section to invite them through iCloud. They need an iCloud account and the app installed. You can stop sharing at any time.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove the data stored on the device. To remove the copy in iCloud, open Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › FreedomPoint and delete its data.
